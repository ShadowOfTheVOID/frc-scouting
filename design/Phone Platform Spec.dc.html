PLATFORM DIFFERENCES THAT MATTER
Back gestureAndroid swipes in from both vertical edges. The rate ladder and hold meter must not use horizontal edge drags — tap and hold only, which is how they are drawn.
Screen wakeKeep-awake is a wake lock on Android and an idle-timer disable on iOS. Both are held for the whole match, released at the buzzer.
HapticsEvery rate change and meter commit fires one short tick. iOS light impact; Android 12ms one-shot. Never a long buzz.
OrientationLocked landscape, both rotations allowed so the phone can be held either way. No portrait layout exists.
Offline storeSame schema on both; queue survives force-quit and reboot. Phone 03 is the state the scout sees while it drains.
