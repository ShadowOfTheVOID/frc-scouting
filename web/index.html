OFF-SITE MIRROR
Optional, and the only thing here that sends your event anywhere. Every minute this hub packs the whole event — teams, matches, scouting, the picklist, the solved numbers — and pushes it to a site your team hosts. Two things come of that: the data survives this laptop, and anyone with the mirror passcode can read the numbers from a phone on cell data, off the venue wifi entirely. It is read-only over there; nothing on the mirror can change anything here. Nothing is sent while the event has not changed.
